LenguaTube privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026

In short: LenguaTube keeps everything on your computer. The developer has no server and receives none of your data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold. Your information leaves your computer only when you use an optional feature that needs another service (such as Gemini voices or Google Drive backups), and then it goes straight from your browser to that service.

What LenguaTube is

LenguaTube is a Chrome extension for learning languages (Spanish, Japanese, French, Italian and English). It shows interactive subtitles on YouTube, Prime Video, Netflix and Crunchyroll, swaps some words on web pages into the language you're learning, and offers practice exercises and flashcard export.

What is stored, and where

All of the following is stored only in your browser on your computer (Chrome extension storage and IndexedDB):

  • Your settings, levels and display preferences.
  • Words and sentences you save, words you mark as known, practice results and daily goals.
  • For saved items: the sentence they came from, and the video or page title and link, so you can go back to them.
  • A short list of recent videos you watched with LenguaTube on (title and ID), used for scores and practice topics.
  • Dictionaries and word lists you choose to install.
  • Any API keys you enter (Gemini, Azure). Keys are never put into backup files.

Uninstalling the extension deletes this data from Chrome.

Web pages you visit

To swap words on web pages, LenguaTube reads the text of the page in your browser. That text is translated by Chrome's built-in translator, which runs on your computer. Page text is not sent to the developer or to any online service. If you save a swapped word, its sentence, the page title and address are stored locally with it. You can turn word swaps off entirely, or for any site.

What the developer receives

Nothing. LenguaTube has no developer-run server, account system, analytics or crash reporting. Your data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything other than the features described here.

Your control

  • Export, back up, restore or delete your data from LenguaTube Settings → Your data and backups.
  • Remove an API key at any time in Settings; the optional services above then stop.
  • Disconnect Google Drive from your Google Account's security settings (third-party access).

Children

LenguaTube is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
